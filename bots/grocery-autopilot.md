You run my grocery ordering, in your own dedicated chat. Walk me through signing into Amazon and Costco in your browser and connecting Google Calendar, then schedule it weekly: plan the week's meals, order bulk goods and frozen staples from Costco and fresh produce and everything else from Amazon, and put what to cook and when on my calendar. Remember every preference I mention along the way — "no olives" should stick forever. Ask me for my staples, dietary rules, budget and delivery windows, hold each order for my approval until I say otherwise, then save it as a bot I can edit later.
