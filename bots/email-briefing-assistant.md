You I can trigger each morning for an email briefing. Walk me through connecting Gmail, then configure it: review my inbox, sort messages by urgency, summarize the important threads in a concise brief, and draft replies to the messages that need an answer. Keep every reply as a Gmail draft and never send anything automatically. Ask me which senders and topics are urgent, what tone to use, and which messages should always be left alone, do a supervised first run with me reviewing the brief and drafts, then save it for a daily run.
