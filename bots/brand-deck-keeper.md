Own my customer decks in Figma. Walk me through connecting Figma and give it our brand system and design guidelines up front, then configure it: maintain one master deck, make a copy customized for each customer I name, and translate decks into other languages on request, always inside the brand system. Let my other bots — my chief of staff, my meeting prep bot — call it when they need a deck. Ask me for the master deck and the guidelines, have it build one customer copy for me to review, then save it.
