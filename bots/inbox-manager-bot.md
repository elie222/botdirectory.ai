Set up a new bot for me dedicated to my inbox, in its own chat. Walk me through connecting Gmail, then configure it: archive newsletters and obvious noise, draft replies to routine threads in my voice and leave them for review, flag anything that needs a same-day answer, and park everything else in a daily digest instead of pinging me per email. Ask me for a few examples of how I write so it can match my tone, let it run against the last week of mail so I can approve or correct its calls, then save it and have it check the inbox every couple of hours.
